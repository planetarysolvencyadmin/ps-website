Planetary Solvency is an independent, non-profit risk management programme founded by the [Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA)](https://actuaries.org.uk/), in partnership with the [University of Exeter's Global Systems Institute](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/research/institutes/gsi/).

We operate as a nexus organisation, bringing together risk professionals, scientists, financial institutions, corporates, policymakers and NGOs around a shared, science-informed approach to systemic risk.

We are hosted by the [Cambridge Institute for Sustainability Leadership (CISL)](https://www.cisl.cam.ac.uk/).

## How Planetary Solvency is different

Planetary Solvency did not originate in the environmental movement. Born out of the actuarial profession, we are anchored in a discipline that holds undisputed credibility in measuring the probability and risk of future events, evaluating levels of financial jeopardy and pricing risk correctly.

We work with existing power, not around it. We are not looking to build a new financial system; rather equip those within it whom boards, governments and pension trustees already trust, to see systemic risk as clearly as they see credit or market risk.

Our ambition then is that capital reallocates at scale: away from activities that erode Earth-system stability and towards those that restore it  – because the risk-adjusted numbers genuinely require it.

With human prosperity dependent on staying within the limits of living systems, our actuarial heritage helps us to express this in the language of risk, capital and solvency that global finance already runs on.

Long term, we hope the economic system itself will evolve to operate within planetary boundaries as a matter of course, not exception; an economy that treats living within nature's limits not as a constraint on prosperity, but as the precondition for it.

## Our Mission

Ensuring planetary solvency to support lasting human prosperity.

## Our Vision

Reconnecting finance and the economy to the biosphere so that humanity lives in balance with nature.
