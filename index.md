# An introduction to Planetary Solvency

Our society and economy fundamentally depend on the Earth’s living systems to provide critical infrastructure such as food, water, energy, raw materials and climate regulation.

Human prosperity depends on staying within the limits of these living systems, with global finance holding trillions in assets whose value depends on the stability of a functioning biosphere and ecosystem services.

Yet the stability of these systems is increasingly threatened by accelerating climate change and nature loss.  Managing human activity to stay within planetary boundaries is not simply a worthy environmental aim; it is a global risk management imperative.

## How can Planetary Solvency help?

Planetary Solvency takes well established financial services risk and solvency management techniques and applies them to the Earth system.

Our intention is to evidence the realistic level of planetary systems risk to financial, economic and policy actors.

To do this, we equip those inside the institutions that hold the reach and credibility required to move global capital — including insurers, finance ministries, asset managers, actuaries and regulators — with frameworks and products to see systemic risk clearly, so they can better understand how to reallocate capital accordingly.

We believe building a shared understanding about the level of risk is crucial. Currently, conventional climate models do not account for tipping points or non-linear, compounding risks - understating physical risk and leaving institutional practice operating outside reasonable risk appetite.

This needs to change. Enabling better biosphere-literate decisions to stay within stable physical limits is crucial to maintaining the solvency of the system upon which our economies and societies rely.
