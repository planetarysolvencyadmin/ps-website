Our Planetary Solvency programme of activity focuses on three audiences:
  
* Financial Services
* Government Entities and Regulators related to finance/ Ministries of Finance
* Corporates
 
## Financial Services
 
Within our financial services channel, we are designing and rolling out activity supporting financial institutions, regulators and policymakers with realistic economic damage estimates for climate and nature. 
We will also be looking at catalysing capital reallocation solutions.
 
 
## Government Entities and Regulators related to finance/ Ministries of Finance


We are building ongoing engagement with Ministry of Finance secretariat and leadership, as we believe these entities have the highest-leverage policy pathway for systemic change. 
Within this channel of activity, we are also working with stakeholders in the policy space, showcasing the FCA Sandbox outputs– aligning with a development group to build Ministry of Finance-relevant outputs. 
 

## Corporates

Widespread systems illiteracy is leaving many business leaders and boards unprepared for a future in which breaching planetary boundaries presents material financial and operational risks: something boards must actively manage. 

Failing to account for nature-related dependencies exposes organisations to severe regulatory, litigation, and transition risks. In our work with this audience, we will be working to bring the suite of Planetary Solvency products to the business community, to help them navigate through this new reality.
  
Across all three audiences, rather than engaging individually with every pension scheme, Ministry or corporate, we look to equip existing trusted intermediaries — actuarial bodies, rating agencies, asset managers, sustainability coalitions — to take Planetary Solvency into their own networks.
